Free online darts scorer for 501, 301 and 701

Darts Scorer keeps score for your darts games straight from the browser. No app store, no account, no ads. Open it on a phone, tablet or laptop, tap the players' names and start throwing. It saves after every visit, so a locked phone or a lost signal never loses the game.

What it does

  • 501, 301 and 701 for 1 to 8 players, first to any number of legs, double-out or straight-out.
  • Checkout suggestions from the standard checkout table, shown for the player at the oche and narrowed to what is finishable with the darts left.
  • Bust detection and validation of impossible scores, so the maths is never wrong on a busy night.
  • Two ways to score: type the visit total, or tap each dart (single, double, treble, miss, 25, bull).
  • Statistics: 3-dart average, 180s, 140+ and 100+ visits, highest checkout, best leg, plus lifetime stats across every match.
  • Undo any visit, even back across a finished leg.

Works offline, installs like an app

Darts Scorer is a progressive web app. After the first visit it loads instantly with no connection. On Android and desktop Chrome an Install button appears on the setup screen; on iPhone use Share then Add to Home Screen. Your players, matches and stats stay on your device, and you can export a backup file from the History screen.

Frequently asked

Is it really free?
Yes. No ads, no premium tier, no sign-up. The source code is open on GitHub.
Does it need an internet connection?
Only the first time. After that it works offline, including in a pub cellar.
Which checkout does it suggest?
The commonly used route for every finish from 2 to 170, such as T20 T20 Bull for 170 or T20 T19 D12 for 141. With one or two darts left it only shows finishes that are still possible.
Where is my data stored?
In your browser on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server. Use Export in History to back it up or move it to another phone.
